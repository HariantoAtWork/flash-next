Enable Flash-Next image inputs by downloading `mmproj-BF16.gguf` and passing `--mmproj` to Gufo serve.

The vision projector is stored under `MMPROJ_HOST_DIR` (default `/var/cache/flash-next`) and bind-mounted at `/mmproj` inside the container (separate from the read-only `/models` volume), so image mode still works when the `/docker` models volume is full.
